Privacy Policy — DailyClipFacts

Last updated: October 15, 2026

DailyClipFacts is a personal automation tool used by its sole owner to upload short videos to their own YouTube channel.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

This application does not collect, store, or share personal information from
any user. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

GOOGLE ACCOUNT ACCESS

The application accesses a single Google account — the owner's — through the
YouTube Data API, using these permissions:
- youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's own channel
- youtube.readonly: to read the owner's own channel and video list
- yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owner's own view statistics

This access is used only to publish and measure the owner's own content. No data is transmitted to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

DATA STORAGE

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer and
are never transmitted anywhere except to Google's own servers for the purposes
described above.

USE OF GOOGLE USER DATA

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API
Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

REVOKING ACCESS

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
